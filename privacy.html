1. Who we are

Daily Accounting Tip is a personal educational content project. Our software ("the App") creates short accounting videos and publishes them to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

2. Information we access

When we authorize the App with TikTok (Login Kit / Content Posting API) or YouTube, it receives an access token for our own account only. The App uses the following data solely to publish our videos:

  • Basic account information (account ID, display name) returned by the platform, to confirm which account is posting.
  • Upload and publish status of the videos we post.

The App does not collect, read, store, or process data about viewers, followers, or any other users. It has no sign-up for the public and no user accounts.

3. How we use information

Access tokens are used only to upload and publish our own videos and to check their publishing status. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and API keys are stored as encrypted secrets in our private hosting environment and are never published. We keep them only while the App is in use and delete them when access is revoked.

5. Your rights

Because the App does not process data about other people, there is no personal data of viewers for us to access, correct, or delete. You may contact us with any privacy question at the address below.

6. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or from Google Account permissions for YouTube.

7. Children

Our content is educational and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
