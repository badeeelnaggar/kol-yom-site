عن القناة

كل يوم بننشر فيديو قصير (حوالي 30 ثانية) فيه حوار بين المحاسب "سامي" والمدير المالي "أبو يوسف"، بيشرح معلومة واحدة من معايير المحاسبة الدولية (IFRS / IAS) بمثال عملي وقيد محاسبي.

About

We publish one short educational video per day explaining a single concept from the International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS / IAS), as a dialogue between an accountant and a finance director, with a worked example and journal entry.

Characters and voices are AI-generated. Content is for education only and is not professional advice.

Our app uses the TikTok and YouTube APIs only to publish our own videos to our own accounts.
